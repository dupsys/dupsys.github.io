Misinformation & Hate-Speech Detection

Detecting harmful and misleading content online, including in low-resource languages.

This line of work studies how to detect misinformation and hateful narratives on social media, including multimodal content and low-resource languages (De Jager et al., 2023; Rananga et al., 2024; du Plessis et al., 2025).
