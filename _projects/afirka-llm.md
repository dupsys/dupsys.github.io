[**Afirka LLM**](https://app.afirkallm.org/#purpose) is an open initiative at the Data Science for Social Impact (DSFSI) lab, University of Pretoria, that builds open data and models for African languages. The project is funded by the Google Research Fund (USD 200,000). I am a **Co-Principal Investigator** on the project, working with Dr Idris Abdulmumin and the DSFSI team.

The project:

- collects, cleans, and verifies African-language web text, together with a community seeding campaign run with Common Crawl;
- standardises and generates instruction data for African contexts;
- adapts open Gemma models for around 40 focus languages;
- builds evaluation benchmarks and a safety-review corpus (AfriGuard) to make the resulting models more **reliable and safe**.

All resources are released openly. Project site: [app.afirkallm.org](https://app.afirkallm.org/#purpose).
