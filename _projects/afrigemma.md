**AfriGemma** is an initiative to advance African-language NLP by building a multilingual, multimodal Pan-African large language model. The initiative received USD 200,000 in support. I am a **Co-Principal Investigator** on the initiative, working with Dr Idris Abdulmumin and collaborators at DSFSI, University of Pretoria.
