**AfriGemma** is an initiative to advance African-language NLP by adapting open Gemma models to African languages and releasing open data, models, and tooling. It is funded by the Google Research Fund (USD 200,000). I am a **Co-Principal Investigator** on the initiative, working with Dr Idris Abdulmumin and collaborators at DSFSI, University of Pretoria.
