Attributing anonymous or disputed social-media posts to their authors is a core task in digital forensics. My PhD research developed regularised deep neural networks and BiLSTM models with subword embeddings that make attribution more accurate and more generalisable on short, noisy text {% cite app12157518 modupe2023integrating %}.
