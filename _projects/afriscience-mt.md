**AfriScience-MT** builds and evaluates machine translation of scientific text into African languages, so that research knowledge is available beyond English. I am a co-author of the EMNLP 2026 paper with Idris Abdulmumin, Vukosi Marivate, and colleagues {% cite abdulmumin2026afriscience %}.
